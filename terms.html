Clark Dashboard Sync: terms of use

Clark Dashboard Sync is a private tool made for one organization, the 23-25 Clark Condominium Trust (Somerville, Massachusetts). It is not offered, sold or licensed to anyone else.

It reads the association's own QuickBooks Online books, read-only, to build a password-protected summary page for the association's unit owners. It never changes anything in QuickBooks.

The summary is provided as is, for the owners' information. The QuickBooks books remain the association's official record.

Questions: contact the association's treasurer.
