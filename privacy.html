Clark Dashboard Sync: privacy policy

Clark Dashboard Sync is a private tool used only by the 23-25 Clark Condominium Trust.

What it reads

Once a night it reads the association's QuickBooks Online reports and transactions (bank balance, income, expenses, owner dues payments), read-only.

What it keeps and shows

It keeps a working copy of those figures on the treasurer's computer and publishes a summary (totals by month and category, dues paid by unit, project payments) to a page that only the association's unit owners can open with a password. The summary leaves out contact details, bank account numbers and anything else not needed for that purpose.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or any other purpose.

Disconnecting

The association can disconnect the tool from QuickBooks at any time in QuickBooks (Settings, Apps). Its saved connection is then deleted.

Questions: contact the association's treasurer.
